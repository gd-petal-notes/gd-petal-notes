Know the facts

Global Impact

Global reach

Breast cancer is the most common cancer in women worldwide, with roughly 2.3 million new cases diagnosed each year.

Health & Prevention

Early detection

Finding breast cancer early provides more treatment options and significantly better outcomes. Talk to a doctor about routine screening.

Important Knowledge

Not only women

Men can develop breast cancer too, though it is much rarer. Everyone of all backgrounds should know the signs.

Sources: World Health Organization · NHS · American Cancer Society. More resources

About & Support

Why Petal Notes?

Created by the Graphic Design Club (GDC) for our breast cancer awareness event. Petal Notes is a calm corner of the internet where people can leave kind words, and where anyone affected can come to read them, privately and any time.

Trusted sources

  • World Health Organization (who.int)
  • NHS (nhs.uk)
  • American Cancer Society (cancer.org)
  • Breastcancer.org

Need to talk?

If you're struggling, please reach out to your doctor, a local cancer support organisation, or someone you trust. You don't have to carry this alone.

This site shares general awareness information and is not medical advice.
